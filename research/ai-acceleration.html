Overview

As AI models continue to grow in scale and complexity, achieving high performance and energy efficiency has become a critical challenge in modern computing systems. We investigate AI acceleration techniques across computing architectures and large-scale AI systems. Our research includes energy-efficient AI computing architectures, communication optimization, workload balancing, and topology-aware computation and data placement for efficient distributed AI processing.
