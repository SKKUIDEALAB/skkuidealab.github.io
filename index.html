Welcome to IDEA LAB

INTELLIGENT DATA-CENTRIC EMERGING ARCHITECTURE LAB

IDEA Lab investigates hardware and software challenges
in DRAM-based memory systems and System-on-Chip architecture.

We are recruiting self-motivated graduates students
who want to explore and uncover new knowledge to change the world.
